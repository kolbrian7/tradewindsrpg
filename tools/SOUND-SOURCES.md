# Background sound sources

All six loops are mixed from the Sonniss GDC Game Audio Bundles (2024 and 2026), licence v2.0:
royalty-free use in commercial games, no attribution required. Not allowed: selling or giving away
the sounds themselves (raw or edited) and using them to train AI. The full library is on the
basement desktop at `D:\Sound Library\Sonniss GDC`.

Built with `tools/make_loops.py` from `tools/recipes.json` (layers mixed, loudness-matched, end crossfaded into the start).

| Loop | Layers |
|---|---|
| port | Jake Fielding, Industrial Harbor: water lapping against port · Rogue Waves, Yacht & Marina: marina, rope creaking |
| market | Sonik Sound Library, Spanish Wallas: dense crowd, exterior · harbour water · marina |
| shipyard | Jake Fielding, Circus Ambiences: hammering, tools · harbour water · marina |
| cantina | Sculptunes, Rome Urban City: indoor pub crowd |
| sea | Rogue Waves, Yacht & Marina: small cabin, hatch open · Epic Stock Media, wind loop · Just Sound Effects, Rocky Coast of Norway: soft waves |
| battle | Nick McMahan, Cascadia Shore Waves: medium to large rocks · 344 Audio, Extreme Winds: eye of the storm |
